# Limitations, Research Boundaries & Open Policy Questions
**Document Series:** Institutional Handover Package (12 of 12)  
**Target Audience:** Parliamentary Health Committees, Research Working Groups, and Public Policy Observers  

---

### 1. Inherent Methodological Limitations of Proposed Models
- **Self-Reported Income Volatility:** Cash-flow and declared-income models require robust verification. In the absence of third-party digital data (e.g. KRA, bank, or telecom integrations), self-declaration carries risk of under-reporting without field CHP verification.
- **Informal Business Capital vs. Profit:** While structured ledgers separate operating expenses from gross receipts, separating household living expenses from micro-enterprise working capital remains difficult in informal trade.
- **Asset Illiquidity vs. Collateral:** Real estate or livestock holdings may not yield monthly liquid cash, but they represent resilience against economic shocks. Completely ignoring physical assets risks regressive targeting.
- **Absence of Real Empirical Household Microdata:** The candidate algorithms have not yet been calibrated against verified empirical household consumption microdata (such as KNBS KIHBS). Superiority cannot be claimed until empirical benchmarking is executed.
- **Administrative Cost of National Scale Field Verification:** While assisted human review solves algorithmic opacity, deploying Community Health Promoters and sub-county officers to audit millions of informal declarations incurs substantial, currently unquantified operational and recurrent administrative costs.

### 2. Open Policy and Legal Questions for SHA Leadership
1. **The Indigent Subsidy Boundary:** What exact per-capita welfare score defines eligibility for complete premium exemption funded by the national government under Section 27(4) of the Social Health Insurance Act, 2023?
2. **Frequency of Means-Testing:** Given high income volatility in the informal sector, how frequently should non-salaried households be re-assessed (e.g. annually, semi-annually, or only upon life events)?
3. **The Role of Community Health Promoters:** What legal protections, statutory authority, and training should be granted to CHPs conducting physical livelihood verifications to ensure compliance with Section 35 of the Kenya Data Protection Act and Regulation 22 of the Data Protection (General) Regulations 2021 regarding meaningful human intervention?
4. **Statutory Reconciliation with Section 27 and Regulations 18 & 21:** What is the formal legal opinion on whether a structured income ledger can be introduced administratively under Regulation 21(2) to establish household income for Regulation 18(1), or whether formal regulatory amendment is required? *(Note: Regulation 21(6) designates the Fifth Schedule, titled 'Means Testing' [Gazette p. 798], as the statutory formula: $\log(y)^u = a^u + b^uX_h^u + c^uZ_{hi}^u + \varepsilon_h^u$; Regulation 17 governs salaried employment; Regulation 18(1)–(2) governs non-salaried with minimum KSh 300 under Reg 18(2); Regulation 18(3) governs annual payment timing; Regulation 18(4)–(6) enables premium financing; Regulation 19 governs indigent financial assistance; Regulation 20 governs lawful custody; Regulation 62 is titled 'Zones').*
5. **Relationship Between Gazetted Income Formula and Operational Consumption Prediction:** The Fifth Schedule specifies an econometric formula estimating log per-capita *income*, while the operational model disclosed by SHA to investigators in November 2025 predicts log per-capita *consumption*. How does SHA's operational instrument formally bridge this conceptual divergence, and what calibration is applied before assigning contributions under Regulation 18(1)?
6. **Judicial Directives under [2026] KEHC 3972 (KLR):** Following the High Court judgment of 19 March 2026 (*Okoiti v CS Health*), which affirmed regulatory validity while ordering corrective action for fair and effective means testing, what formal administrative timeline has the Ministry established to review means-testing procedures, and does any pending appeal affect this directive?
