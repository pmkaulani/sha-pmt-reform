### Mandatory Multi-Domain Acceptance Standards
Full operational deployment requires formal verification across 5 domains:
1. **Methodological Rigour:** Independent validation completed under frozen protocols; no unsupported claims; affordability decoupled from accuracy.
2. **Legal & Regulatory Clearance:** Statutory lawful basis formally determined (DPA Sec 30); DPIA approved under DPA Sec 31; substantive human review safeguards verified under Data Protection (General) Reg 22; SHI Regulations 18 & 21 reconciliation established.
3. **Cybersecurity & Architecture:** Independent penetration test passed; air-gap isolation verified; zero core write-access confirmed.
4. **Operational Readiness:** 11 governance roles formally assigned; reviewer SOPs published; 8-step rollback drill successfully tested.
5. **Institutional Sign-Off:** Unanimous sign-off from SHA Executive Sponsor, Technical Owner, Policy Owner, and Independent Oversight Board.

*Note: All numerical thresholds (e.g. latency, discrepancy rates) must be formally approved by policy owners and are marked `NOT_POLICY_APPROVED` until institutional sign-off.
