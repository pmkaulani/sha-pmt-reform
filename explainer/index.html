The Solution

Adjustable Gross Income (AGI) Model

Replaces housing proxy guesswork with digitally verifiable income signals. Same statutory 2.75% rate — no legislation needed. Just a better tax base.

🔺

Triangulated Verification

Cross-references KRA income data, NTSA vehicle registry, and Safaricom M-Pesa retained balance. Three independent signals audit every self-declaration.

🛡️

8 Vulnerability Exemptions

PWDs (30% deduction), Bodabodas (50% commercial), Seasonal workers, Chama treasurers, Diaspora, Refugees, SACCO savers, and Consent-withheld pathway.

🚩

18-Flag Fraud Engine

Detects phantom dependents, hidden vehicles, income splitting, multi-SIM concealment, and false commercial claims — all automated, no housing inspections.

📊

SHAP Deduction Receipts

Every household gets a plain-language receipt explaining exactly why their premium is what it is. Full transparency, constitutional Art. 35 compliance.

🏙️

3-Tier Cost of Living

Nairobi/Mombasa/Kisumu (KSh 18K), secondary cities (KSh 10K), rural (KSh 4K). Actual rent deducted up to a capped ceiling — not a flat assumed value.

⚖️

Full Legal Compliance

Constitution Art. 27/43/54/56, DPA 2019, AI Bill 2026, SHA Act 2023 §27. No legislative amendment required — the model changes the base, not the rate.
